# BETTY — Less screen. More road.

An offline-capable, zero-build, interactive product experience prototype.

**Run:** Open `index.html` in a modern browser. Google Fonts are optional; fallback fonts work without a connection. For browser speech, click an orange Betty orb or step through a speaking moment with the prototype controls. Speech synthesis depends on platform/browser voice availability.

**Try:** Start ride → Next moment (7 scenarios) → Pause ride → Why that moment? → End ride → Ride story. Other flows: readiness disclosure, route choice, Ask Betty simulation, Journeys, Explore and personality settings.

**Demo limitations:** GPS, route progress, weather, connected Cardo headset, riding telemetry, journey statistics, spoken content and landmark cues are simulated. This is not a live navigation device or safety-warning system. Voice uses browser text-to-speech; Ask Betty is a rule-driven text simulation and does not use speech recognition or a live LLM. Do not interact with a phone while riding.

**Design principles:** Voice as centre of gravity; one insight at a time; low-contrast scenic quiet; relevant information rises into view only on demand; progressive disclosure while stopped; no rigid dashboard; one orange signature cue. Influenced by Dieter Rams' principles, Beeline's focused Moto II display, Royal Enfield Tripper's paired analog/digital approach, and the tactile industrial warmth of motorcycle switchgear. Original independent concept, no affiliate relationship.

Brand exploration and citations are in `brand-study.html`.
