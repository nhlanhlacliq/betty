01 / A position

THE ANTI-
DASHBOARD.

The motorcycle has a dashboard already. Betty does not need to become a second one. She offers situational intelligence, a human-centred voice, and an optional visual cue that supports the moment.

Promise: Notice the right things. Say them well. Leave the rest alone.

02 / A personality

GOOD COMPANY.
NO NONSENSE.

Competent, dry-witted and quietly observant. Useful before clever, warm without being chirpy. The personality is expressed in timing, not an endless stream of jokes.

“Some moments deserve a story.”
“OTHERS DESERVE THE ROAD.”
03 / The visual signature

TACTILE, NOT TECHY.

Borrow the confidence of physical instruments: dark matte cockpit surfaces, a warm dial-like voice presence, condensed road-sign typography and tiny monospaced instrument readouts. Cream offers breathing room away from the road; orange signals Betty herself.

04 / Behaviour beats decoration
01. ONE THING AT A TIME.

One statement, one supporting visual, then back to calm. Weather and geography combine into a single coherent thought.

02. THREE DEPTHS.

Voice while moving. Glanceable cue on the mount. Full details only when parked or deliberately requested.

03. SILENCE HAS DESIGN.

Idle does not mean loading. When Betty is quiet, the screen should look intentionally finished.

04. THE RIDER GOES FIRST.

Content never outweighs route uncertainty, an important alert, a phone call or the desire to just ride.

05. IMMEDIATELY LEGIBLE.

Large type, restrained colour, unambiguous status. Small annotations are for stationary exploration only.

06. EARN EVERY PIXEL.

No permanently visible dashboards of stats, controls, timelines and explanations. Reveal them as needed.

05 / Reference study · research inputs, not visual copies

LOOKING OUTSIDE
THE SCREEN.

Dieter Rams / Braun. Utility, self-explanation, longevity, and “as little design as possible”. Take the discipline, not a sterile colour palette. Primary principles ↗

Beeline Moto II. A compelling example of route guidance reduced to the next useful cue. Take the glanceability, not its circular hardware shell or proprietary interface. Product reference ↗

Royal Enfield Tripper. Separates a restrained instrument-oriented mode from richer map details. Take the concept of contextual mode selection. Product reference ↗

Teenage Engineering. A useful study in making digital products feel tangible and likeable with simple physical cues and typographic precision. This is an interpretive design reference, not an endorsement or a sourced description of exact brand assets. Official website ↗

Motorcycling safety context. A simulated motorcycling study found navigation in data glasses was recognised faster than head-down displays; no finding here proves this prototype is safer. The sensible design response is to keep the moving interface minimal and validate it in actual riding conditions. Research abstract ↗
